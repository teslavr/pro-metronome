Frequently asked questions

I bought “Hide Ads” in an earlier version. Do I have to pay again for Pro?
No. Open the app, tap Pro or the ⓘ next to the title, then Restore purchases. Your earlier purchase unlocks Metronome Pro.

What is free and what is Pro?
The metronome, tap tempo, accents, subdivisions, presets, the Rock and Disco grooves and two rudiments are free. Metronome Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks every groove, the brushes, 808 and 909 drum kits, all rudiments and extra click sounds.

How do I play along to a groove?
Tap Drums on the main screen, choose a groove and press play. The groove follows your tempo; switch on “Click on every beat” if you also want the click.

The app is silent.
Check the volume and that the ring/silent switch is not muting the phone. The metronome plays in the background and together with other music apps.

My device is older than iOS 17.
Version 3.0 needs iOS 17 or later. On older devices the App Store offers the last compatible version.
